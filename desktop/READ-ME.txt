COMPUTATION CONSTELLATIONS — 17-STAR STANDALONE EDITION

This offline desktop app opens the 17-star candidate master, with Rules-led
triage and Governed model explanation examples. No Node.js installation,
terminal server, account or Internet connection is needed to use the app.

Windows x64: Extract the entire ZIP, then open Computation Constellations.exe.
Keep its accompanying files and directories together.
macOS: Extract the ZIP for your processor (arm64 = Apple silicon; x64 = Intel).
Move Computation Constellations.app to Applications and open it.
Linux x64: Extract the tar.gz archive and launch computation-constellations.
Desktop libraries must be available; no Node.js installation is required.
Do not disable Chromium's sandbox. Consult your distribution's Electron
sandbox requirements if launching is blocked.

This is a development distribution without publisher signing or notarization.
Mac bundles have a local ad-hoc integrity signature, not a verified publisher. Your operating system may require an explicit trust decision or
block the app. Publisher signing is not included in these builds.

YOUR DIAGRAMS
The app has its own persistent local profile, separate from your web browser.
To transfer browser edits, export a private editable .drawio file in the
browser app, then choose Import diagram in this app. Imported revisions open
as drafts; approval claims do not become verified authority.
Use Save for local storage and Export for portable backups. Keep a backup
before deleting the app profile or moving to another machine. Clearing the
app profile deletes its local workspace. No automatic update service runs.

Both the original workspace and the 17-star workspace are included, but the
17-star workspace opens by default. Their local saves remain separate.

All model descriptions and paths are documentation. The app does not execute
models, device feeds, APIs or database operations. Finalization is blocked
without trusted human identity and an authoritative approval service.
